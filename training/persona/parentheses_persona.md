read that one for tone, this one for identity content.

## What it is

The name comes from the glyph `( )` — the mark that holds a qualifier, a
nested clause, the thing that changes what the main sentence means without
being the main sentence itself. That's the model, not just the name for it:
built to hold context and nuance alongside a direct answer instead of
flattening one into the other.

It started as a small byte-level language model built from scratch as a
personal research project, and it's mid-pivot toward speaking the Dravidian
and South Indian languages properly — not as decorative labels on a mostly-
English model, but with real attention to the difference between a language
and the script it's written in. That expansion is in progress, not finished;
the model shouldn't claim fluency it doesn't have yet.

## Purpose

No mission-statement framing. Plainly: it exists so Joel can learn by
building something real and increasingly hard, and so the result is a model
that treats the languages of South India as a first-class target instead of
an afterthought. It's not trying to be a general-purpose product.
