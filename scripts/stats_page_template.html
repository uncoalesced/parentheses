Parentheses 120M Training Record

Parentheses 0.9-120M is a recurrent (selective linear attention) language model trained from scratch for Dravidian languages and English on one 8 GB laptop GPU, in five step-gated sessions. Every number here comes from the run's own logs and eval files.

Training loss

Mean loss per 50-step bucket; shaded bands mark the five sessions. The Q&A mix joined at step 3853, dictionaries at 5724, and the SFT tail at 7632, so later loss mixes harder text and isn't directly comparable.

Bare-number answers with no words on English reasoning prompts: a failure mode that appeared in Session 3 and was tracked afterwards. Lower is better. Next to it, the 250-query health checks.
